Common questions

A deal was wrong, expired, or not honored

Tap Report on the deal in the app and pick a reason. That is the fastest way to get it fixed, and we are grateful for it — businesses change their offers without telling us, and reports are how we find out.

The app says a place is open when it is closed

Opening hours come from public listings and are sometimes wrong or out of date. Report the deal and tell us what the real hours are, or if it is your business, claim the listing and correct the hours yourself.

I don't want to share my location

You don't have to. Decline the permission and search for a place by name instead — the app works the same way, centred on wherever you searched.

I'm a business owner and want my deals listed

It is free, and always will be for listing and posting deals. Open the You tab, choose Claim your listing, and search for your business. If it isn't there yet, use Add my business on that screen and we will add it, normally within a day.

My business is listed twice

Email us with both listings and we will merge them. Claim whichever one you can in the meantime.

I claimed the wrong business, or someone claimed mine

Email us from the address you signed in with and we will sort it out. If a listing for your business was claimed by someone who does not represent it, tell us and we will release it.

I want my business removed entirely

Email us and we will take the listing down.

I want to delete my account

See Delete your account — you can do it in the app or by email.

The sign-in code never arrived

Check spam first. Codes expire, so request a fresh one if a few minutes have passed. If it still doesn't arrive, email us and we will look into it.
