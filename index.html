How it works

Closest first

Deals are sorted by how far away they are, not by who paid the most.

On right now

The feed knows what's live at this minute, what starts later today, and what's on all week.

No tracking

Your location is used to find nearby deals and is never stored. No ad SDKs, no profile.

Free to list

Are you a business?

Listing your business and posting deals is free, with no card required. Add yourself below and we'll get you listed — usually within a day. Once you're in, you can claim your listing in the app and post and edit deals yourself.

We only store your email so we can reply about your listing.

Something wrong with a deal?

Businesses change their offers without telling us. Tap Report on any deal in the app, or email us — it's the fastest way to get it fixed and we're grateful for it.
