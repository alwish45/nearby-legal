For businesses

Reach the people who are already nearby.

near.by shows locals and visitors what's on around them right now, sorted by distance. Your business may already be listed. Claim it to post your own specials and keep them accurate.

Free to claim. Free to post.

There is no charge to claim your listing or post deals. Optional featured placement is available; email us and we'll tell you the price, duration and placement in writing before anything is charged.

What you get

  • Post specials, events and trivia nights — one-time, recurring, or always-on.
  • Show up by distance — people near you see you first, whether they're on Main Street or downtown.
  • See results — how many people viewed each deal and tapped for directions. Totals only; we never show you anything about individual people.
  • Fix what's wrong — update hours, menu link and deal details yourself.

How to claim your listing

  1. Download near.by on iPhone or Android.
  2. Open the You tab and tap Claim your listing.
  3. Enter your work email. We'll send a 6-digit code; there's no password.
  4. Find your business and confirm you're authorized to manage it.
  5. Post your first deal. It appears for nearby customers right away.

Questions

Where did my current listing come from?
Listings start from publicly available business information. If something is wrong, claim the listing and fix it, or report it in the app and we'll correct it.

Can I remove my listing?
Yes. Email us from an address associated with the business and we'll take it down.

What can't I post?
Offers you don't intend to honor, or anything that breaks local law. Alcohol promotions must follow Utah's rules. See the Terms of Use.
