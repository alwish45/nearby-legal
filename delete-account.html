Delete your account

Only business owners have near.by accounts. If you just browse deals, there's no account and nothing to delete.

Delete it in the app

  1. Open near.by and go to the You tab.
  2. Sign in with your email code if you aren't already.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

Or ask us by email

Email alandlipp@gmail.com from the address you signed in with, with the subject "Delete my near.by account". We'll delete it within 30 days and reply to confirm.

What is deleted

  • Your account and email address — permanently.
  • Your link to any business you claimed. The business is released so its rightful owner can claim it later.

What stays

  • The business listing and its deals, because they describe a public business rather than you.
  • Anonymous view and tap counts, which were never linked to any person.
