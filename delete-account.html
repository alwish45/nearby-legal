Delete your account

For the near.by mobile app, operated by Near.by LLC (Utah). Last updated 19 September 2026.

You only have a near.by account if you are a business owner who signed in to claim a listing. Browsing deals does not create an account.

Delete it in the app

  1. Open near.by and go to the You tab.
  2. Choose Delete account.
  3. Confirm. The account and the email address attached to it are removed immediately.

Delete it without the app

Email alandlipp@gmail.com from the address you signed in with, with the subject Delete my near.by account.

We will delete it and reply to confirm, normally within a few days and always within 30 days. If you email from a different address we may ask you to confirm ownership of the account first, so that nobody else can delete it.

What is deleted, and what stays

DataWhat happens
Your account and email address Deleted. This is the only personal information we hold about you.
The link between you and any business you claimed Deleted. The business is released so it can be claimed again later.
The business listing and its deals Kept. These describe a public business rather than you, and other people rely on them. Ask us if you want a deal you posted taken down as well — we will remove it.
Anonymous view and tap counts Kept. They were never linked to you or to any individual — they are totals only.

Deletion is permanent. We do not keep a copy of your account after it is deleted, other than anything we are legally required to retain.
