Privacy Policy

For the near.by mobile app, operated by Near.by LLC (Utah). Last updated 29 August 2026.

near.by shows you deals and specials at businesses near your current location. This policy explains exactly what the app collects, why, and what it does not do. It is written to describe the app's actual behavior rather than to cover every hypothetical.

The short version

  • Your location is used on your device to find nearby deals. We do not store your location.
  • We do not track you across apps or websites, and we do not sell data.
  • There are no third-party advertising or analytics SDKs in the app.
  • You only give us an email address if you are a business owner who chooses to sign in.

What the app collects

DataWhyKept?

Deal reports If you report a deal as wrong or expired, we record which deal and the reason you selected. Yes. Reports are anonymous and are not linked to you.

What the app does not collect

  • No advertising identifier (IDFA / AAID), and no tracking across other apps or websites.
  • No contacts, photos, camera, microphone, calendar, or health data.
  • No background location. Location is requested only while the app is open.
  • No browsing history, and no persistent device fingerprint.
  • No third-party advertising, attribution, or analytics SDKs.

Location permission

When you first open near.by, your device asks whether to share your location. If you decline, the app still works — you can search for a place by name instead and browse deals there. You can change this at any time in your device settings.

Who we share data with

We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it for advertising.

The app stores its data with Supabase, which hosts our database and handles sign-in on our behalf. They process this data only to provide that service to us.

Maps are rendered using Apple Maps on iOS and Google Maps on Android. When a map is displayed, the map provider receives the information it needs to draw it, under its own privacy policy (Apple, Google).

When you tap to get directions, call a business, or open its website, your device hands off to that app or site, which is then governed by its own terms.

We may disclose information if we are legally required to.

Business accounts

If you claim a business listing, your email is linked to that business so we know you manage it. Anonymous view and tap counts for your own deals become visible in your dashboard. You never see information about individual people — only totals.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account from inside the app: open the You tab and choose Delete account. This permanently removes your account and email address. Any business you claimed is released so another owner can claim it later; the business listing and its deals remain in the app, since they describe a public business rather than you.

If you have trouble, email alandlipp@gmail.com and we will remove it for you.

Children

near.by is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export, or delete the personal information we hold about you. Because the only personal information we hold is the email address of a business account, deleting your account satisfies all of these. For anything else, email us.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, we will update the date at the top and, where the change is significant, note it in the app.
