Privacy Policy

For the near.by mobile app and the near.by plan pages friends open from a link, operated by Near.by LLC (Utah). Last updated 27 November 2026.

near.by shows you deals, specials and events near you, and helps you make plans with friends. This policy explains exactly what we collect, why, and what we don't do. It is written to describe how near.by actually works rather than to cover every hypothetical.

The short version

  • Your location is used to find what's near you. We keep only a coarse version (rounded to about a third of a mile), tied to a random code that changes each time you open the app, and delete it after 90 days.
  • You don't need an account. The app makes a random ID for your phone so things like votes, reports and "once a day" limits work without one. It isn't linked to your name, your phone's hardware or any advertising ID.
  • Sponsored posts are chosen on your phone. What you browse, save and follow is used there to pick relevant posts and is not sent to us or to businesses.
  • We do not track you across apps or websites, and we do not sell or share data for advertising. There are no third-party advertising or analytics SDKs.

What we don't collect

  • No advertising identifier (IDFA / AAID), and no tracking across other apps or websites.
  • No contacts, photos, camera, microphone, calendar or health data. (Adding a plan to your calendar opens your calendar app; we don't read it.)
  • No background location. Location is requested only while the app is open.
  • No browsing history and no device fingerprint.
  • No third-party advertising, attribution or analytics SDKs.

Sponsored posts

Businesses can pay to show a deal to people near them, labeled "Sponsored". A business chooses an area, times and days, and kinds of things (for example food or live music). Our server sends your phone the sponsored posts for your area; your phone decides which fit what you're browsing and the places you saved or follow, and shows at most about one per eight listings. We are told which post was shown and whether it was tapped or hidden, with your app ID, so it can be counted and charged once a day per phone. We never tell a business who you are, and we don't build a profile of you on our servers. Tap ⋯ on any sponsored post to see why it was shown or to hide it.

Location permission

When you first open near.by, your phone asks whether to share your location. If you decline, the app still works: it shows downtown Salt Lake City, or you can search for a place. You can change this any time in your phone's settings.

Who we share data with

We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it for advertising. We use these service providers, who process data only to provide their service to us:

  • Supabase hosts our database, sign-in and server functions.
  • Stripe processes payments for promoted posts and near.by Pro. Card details go directly to Stripe; we never see or store your full card number.
  • Anthropic (our AI provider) processes the text you give "Plan my night", the text a business owner gives "Fill it in for me", support emails (to draft replies a person reviews), and public business information we check. It does not receive your location history or app ID.
  • Twilio sends text messages to people who ask for plan updates by text.
  • Expo (and Apple and Google) deliver notifications using your notification token.
  • Maps are drawn by Apple Maps on iOS and Google Maps on Android, under their own privacy policies.

Some business details, such as ratings, hours and photos, come from third-party listing services and the businesses themselves; we send those services the business we're looking up, not information about you. Press Pass and Loyalty & rewards link to programs run by others under their own policies. When you tap for directions, to call or to open a website, your phone hands off to that app or site.

We may disclose information if the law requires it. If near.by is acquired or merges with another company, the information we hold may be transferred to it, bound by this policy for that information.

Business accounts

If you claim a business listing, your email is linked to that business. To check that you represent it, we collect the contact name, role, phone number and message you give with your claim, and may check your business's website or DNS records for a verification code. We may use automated tools, including AI, to screen claims for fraud. This is used only to review the claim and is kept as a record of who verified the listing.

Your dashboard shows totals for your own deals and promoted posts: views, saves, taps, hides, spend and an estimate of how many people browse an area. You never see information about individual people.

If you post an update to your followers, the text is shown on your place's page and sent to followers as a notification. If you buy promoted posts or near.by Pro, we keep campaign, credit, subscription and payment records (through Stripe) for accounting and tax, even after a campaign or subscription ends.

Sales rep accounts

If you join the sales rep program, we store your name, email, rep code, who recruited you (if anyone), the businesses linked to your code, the commissions you earned and the payouts we made, and the payment and tax details needed to pay you. Payout and commission records are kept as long as the law requires, even if you delete your account. Businesses you refer may see your display name. A business that enters your code is linked to you; you can see its name and the commission you earn from its payments, but not its card details or account email.

Deleting your account and your data

You can delete your account in the app: open the You tab and choose Delete account. This removes your account and email. A business you claimed is released so another owner can claim it; the listing and its deals stay, since they describe a public business rather than you.

Without an account, deleting the app removes its ID, your saved deals and follows from your phone. To remove plans, names or notes you added, a phone number, or anything else, email nearbyutah@gmail.com and tell us the plan link or what to remove.

Children

near.by is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. Some listings involve alcohol and are meant for adults 21 and over.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (including under the Utah Consumer Privacy Act), you may have the right to know what we hold about you, to get a copy, to correct it, to delete it, and to opt out of targeted advertising and the sale of data. We don't sell data, and sponsored posts are matched on your phone rather than from a profile we keep; you can hide any sponsored post. To use any of these rights, email us; we will answer within 45 days. Records we must keep for tax or accounting are kept only as long as required.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, we will update the date at the top and, where the change is significant, note it in the app.
