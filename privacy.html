Privacy Policy

For the near.by mobile app, operated by Near.by LLC (Utah). Last updated 21 November 2026.

near.by shows you deals and specials at businesses near your current location. This policy explains exactly what the app collects, why, and what it does not do. It is written to describe the app's actual behavior rather than to cover every hypothetical.

The short version

  • Your location is used to find nearby deals. We keep only a coarse version of it (rounded to about a third of a mile), tied to a random session code rather than to you, and delete it after 90 days.
  • We do not track you across apps or websites, and we do not sell data.
  • There are no third-party advertising or analytics SDKs in the app.
  • You only give us an email address if you choose to sign in, for example as a business owner or a near.by sales rep.

What the app does not collect

  • No advertising identifier (IDFA / AAID), and no tracking across other apps or websites.
  • No contacts, photos, camera, microphone, calendar, or health data.
  • No background location. Location is requested only while the app is open.
  • No browsing history, and no persistent device fingerprint.
  • No third-party advertising, attribution, or analytics SDKs.

Location permission

When you first open near.by, your device asks whether to share your location. If you decline, the app still works — you can search for a place by name instead and browse deals there. You can change this at any time in your device settings.

Who we share data with

We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it for advertising.

The app stores its data with Supabase, which hosts our database and handles sign-in on our behalf. They process this data only to provide that service to us.

Payments for promoted deals are processed by Stripe. Your card details go directly to Stripe; we never see or store your full card number.

Maps are rendered using Apple Maps on iOS and Google Maps on Android. When a map is displayed, the map provider receives the information it needs to draw it, under its own privacy policy (Apple, Google).

When you tap to get directions, call a business, or open its website, your device hands off to that app or site, which is then governed by its own terms.

We may disclose information if we are legally required to.

If near.by is acquired by or merged with another company, the information we hold may be transferred to that company, which will remain bound by this policy for that information.

Business accounts

If you claim a business listing, your email is linked to that business so we know you manage it. To check that you really represent the business, we collect the contact name, role, phone number, and message you provide with your claim, and we may check your business's own website or DNS records for a verification code. We may also use automated tools to screen claims for fraud. This information is used only to review the claim and is kept as a record of who verified the listing.

Anonymous view, save, and tap counts for your own deals and promoted deals are visible in your dashboard, along with an estimate of how many people browse the area you choose to promote in. You never see information about individual people — only totals.

If you buy a promoted deal, we keep a record of the campaign, its price, and payment status for accounting and tax purposes, even after the campaign ends.

Sales rep accounts

If you join the near.by sales rep program, we store your name, email, rep code, who recruited you (if anyone), the businesses linked to your code, the commissions you earned, and the payouts we made to you. We may also ask for the payment and tax details required to pay you. Payout and commission records are kept as long as the law requires for tax and accounting, even if you delete your account. Businesses you refer may see your display name.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account from inside the app: open the You tab and choose Delete account. This permanently removes your account and email address. Any business you claimed is released so another owner can claim it later; the business listing and its deals remain in the app, since they describe a public business rather than you.

If you have trouble, email alandlipp@gmail.com and we will remove it for you.

Children

near.by is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export, or delete the personal information we hold about you. For most people, the only personal information we hold is the email address on an account, and deleting your account takes care of it. Records we must keep for tax or accounting (such as payments and rep payouts) are kept only as long as required. For anything else, email us.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, we will update the date at the top and, where the change is significant, note it in the app.
